## 4.1 Small Prototype

### 4.2.1 Overview
![[tet_vs_width.png]]
从整体上来说，走廊宽度对总疏散时间（TET）的影响呈现显著的边际收益递减规律：在 1.0–1.6/1.8 m 区间内，三种布局的 TET 随宽度增加快速下降，而在 ≥2.0 m 后降幅趋缓。该现象与行人流的容量–密度关系一致，表明增大走廊宽度首先缓解了高密度引发的侧向摩擦与微观冲突，从而显著提升通行效率。
### 4.2.2 Speed and Trajectories Analysis 
随着宽度从 1.0 m 增至 1.6–1.8 m，三种布局的主走廊普遍从一条拥挤带，转变为两条相对稳定的并行车道。速度图上表现为：原本连续的红黄带被两条更“绿”的条带取代，慢速区显著收缩。这说明宽度的增加首先减少了侧向摩擦和频繁避让，个体能更长时间保持接近desired speed。对应到总疏散时间，这一阶段的降幅最大，且各布局的结果波动同步变小。

As the width increased from 1.0m to 1.6-1.8m, the main corridors of the three layouts generally transformed from a crowded zone to two relatively stable parallel lanes. The speed chart shows that the originally continuous red and yellow bands have been replaced by two greener bands, and the slow zone has significantly contracted. This indicates that the increase in width first reduces lateral friction and frequent evasion, allowing agents to maintain the desired speed for a longer period. In terms of the total evacuation time, this stage has the largest reduction, and the result fluctuations of each layout have simultaneously decreased.

布局 A 的主走廊较直，沿线存在多个支流汇入点。轨迹与速度图显示，支流在到达汇入口之前已形成有序的队列，进入主走廊时仅出现短距离的轻度减速。由此推断，冲突在支流内部被提前消化，在corridor width 1.6 m 起即可稳定形成双车道，2.0–2.4 m 仅在最终出口前保留小范围减速区。结果是中高宽度段的平均时间较低、方差较小，速度上限较高。并且随着宽度的增加，撤离时间较为稳定地下降。
The main corridor in Layout A is relatively straight, with multiple tributary inflow points along the line. The trajectory and velocity diagrams show that tributaries have already formed orderly queues before reaching the confluence point, with only mild short-distance deceleration when entering the main corridor. This suggests that conflicts are resolved in advance within the tributaries, with stable dual lanes forming starting from corridor widths of 1.6 m, while widths of 2.0–2.4 m maintain only small deceleration zones before the final exit. As a result, medium to high-width sections have lower average times, smaller variances, and higher speed limits. Furthermore, as width increases, evacuation time decreases relatively steadily.

布局 B 含多处 T字或十字交叉与分散的汇入点。在 1.0–1.4 m corridor width情况下，下半部分交叉点附近反复出现队伍断裂与重新组队现象，在图中的直观体现就是形成了多个网状慢速带，拥堵点数量多、范围广。加宽至 1.6–1.8 m 后，这些网状慢速带明显消减，并且形成车道，说明加宽能有效稀释交互冲突，因此时间降幅最大。但到 2.4 m 仍可见残留的浅黄带，导致其高宽度端的速度上限略低于 A 与 C。
Layout B contains multiple T-shaped or cross intersections with dispersed confluence points. Under corridor widths of 1.0-1.4 m, the lower section near intersection points repeatedly experiences team breaking and regrouping phenomena. The visual manifestation in the diagram is the formation of multiple mesh-like low-speed zones, with numerous congestion points covering a wide range. After widening the width to 1.6 and 1.8 m, these mesh-like low-speed zones significantly diminish and form lanes, which indicates that widening the corridor width can effectively avoid interactive conflicts, resulting in the greatest time reduction. However, at 2.4 m, some remaining light yellow zones are still visible, causing the speed limitation at the high-width stage to be slightly lower than layouts A and C.

布局 C 的上游采用平行通道，代理在并入主走廊前能形成较整齐的队列，秩序优于 B。然而在接近主入口前仍需经由明显转角并入。从图4.1可以看到，布局 C 在 1.6 m 时出现了反常波动，撤离时间反而比1.4m走廊宽度要长。我们对比1.4m和1.6m的speed and trajectory 图 Fig 4.4，可以看到1.6m情况下在出口前瓶颈以及下方并道区域内，虽尝试形成两股车道，但两车道中心距仍然偏近，这直观上导致车道宽度大幅增加，反映出了较多的摩擦与避让；这与Social Force Model对邻近行人之间的排斥压力增大的描述一致。宽度提升到 1.8–2.0 m 后，车道间距与并入扩散角增大，慢速带被消解，撤离性能快速提升。到 2.4 m，转角影响基本可忽略，速度上限与 A 接近。
Layout C employs parallel lanes upstream, which allows agents to form queues before merging into the main corridor, showing better organization than Layout B. However, merging still requires navigating through a turning angle, resulting in a relatively low overall speed limitation. As shown in Figure 4.1, Layout C exhibits an abnormal fluctuation at 1.6 m width, where evacuation time is actually longer than that of the 1.4 m corridor width. Comparing the speed and trajectory diagrams (Fig 4.4) for 1.4 m and 1.6 m cases, we can observe that in the 1.6 m scenario, although attempts are made to form two lanes in the bottleneck area before the exit and the merging zone below, the centerline distance between the two lanes remains too close. This intuisively results in a great increase in lane width, reflecting more friction and avoidance behavior, which matches the Social Force Model's description. After the width increases to 1.8-2.0 m, the inter-lane spacing and merging diffusion angle increase, the slow-speed zones are eliminated, and evacuation performance rapidly improves. At the width of 2.4 m , the turning angle effect becomes essentially negligible, and the speed limitation exceeded that of Layout A.

三种布局在以下位置最容易出现稳定的慢速带：交汇/并道口、急转角、最终出口前的收敛段。宽度增加会让这些慢速带变短、变浅、出现次数变少。这可能意味着corridor width并不是直接因素，与其继续盲目加宽，不如优先针对这些慢速带进行优化，例如延长并道过渡、减小并入角度、增加导向与单向组织、局部直线化转角。
Among the three layout types, stable low-speed zones most commonly appear at the following locations: intersections or merging areas, sharp turns and convergence sections before exits. Increasing width makes these low-speed zones shorter, shallower and less frequent. This may indicate that corridor width is not a direct contributing factor. Rather than continuing to widen corridors blindly when the corridor width is already relatively wide, it would be better to optimize these low-speed zones, such as extending merging transitions, reducing merge angles and increasing directional guidance and one-way organization.
### 4.1.2 Double Exits Comparison

根据图5，设置双出口后，三种布局的总疏散时间整体下移，试次间波动显著减小。1.0–1.6/1.8 m 仍是效率提升最快的区间；当宽度超过 2.0 m，继续加宽的边际收益变小，曲线趋于平缓。与单出口相比，双出口主要削弱了末端收敛段的慢速带，使系统更接近容量充足的稳定状态。在双出口条件下，三种布局的速度轨迹更均匀且更连续，绿色高速段显著增多，而红黄慢速带被压缩在更短的局部区域。出发后个体更早分流，主走廊上的回折与蛇形明显减少。
According to Figure 5, after setting up dual exits, the total evacuation time for all three layouts decreased overall, with significantly reduced fluctuation between trials. The 1.0–1.6/1.8 m range remains the interval with the fastest efficiency improvement; when the width beyond 2.0 m, the marginal benefits of continued widening disappear, and the curve tends to flatten. Compared to single exit, double exits first reduce the low-speed zones in the terminal convergence section, offering the system a stable state with sufficient capacity. Under double exits condition, the speed and trajectories of all three layouts are more uniform and the trend is smooth, with significantly more green sections, while red and yellow low-speed zones are compressed into shorter localized areas. Agents seperate earlier after they start to evacute, and the backtracking and snake-like movement in the main corridor are notably reduced.![[All_tet_vs_width.png]]

单出口条件下的 Layout A 在终端出口前形成一段连续的减速带，尽管在中部已出现较为稳定的双车道，但末端车道的收敛，仍然导致速度明显下降并带来了一定波动。设置双出口后，因为形成了两侧分流，汇入点向两侧出口偏移，慢速带被显著削弱，汇入前只剩短促的轻度减速。轨迹上几乎看不到回堵和蛇形，绿色主带贯穿全程。结果是 A2 在各宽度下都能维持低且稳定的总时间，说明双出口直接消除了终端瓶颈限制。
Under single exit condition, Layout A forms a continuous low-speed zone before the exit. Although relatively stable double lanes have appeared in the middle section, the convergence of lanes at the end still causes significant speed reduction and creates certain fluctuations. When it comes to double exits, the flow distribution shifts the merging points towards both side exits, weakening the low-speed zone and leaving deceleration before merging. The trajectories show almost no congestion or fluctuation, with the main green flow band extending throughout the entire length. The result shows that the Layout A2 maintains low and stable total times across all widths, showing that the double-exit design directly eliminates the terminal bottleneck.

单出口的 B 在多个交叉附近反复出现队伍断裂与重新组队现象，速度图上形成网状的红黄慢速带，尤其在 1.0–1.4 m 表现突出。引入第二出口后，B2 的终端收敛压力并没有显著减轻，时间下降较为轻微，甚至在1.2m和1.4-1.8m段时间有所上升。其原因可能是在上半部分区域仍然保留了多交叉点，代理需要多次绕行和重排，而双侧分流和加宽通道并不能有效缓解这种局部交叉点的拥堵，同时较宽的通道反而可能使得代理的avoidance behavior更为明显，从而拖慢了撤离时间。说明对于交叉密集的几何，双出口虽然能够缓解末端拥堵，但要进一步缩短慢速带，还需要依靠局部的几何整形措施。
In single-exit Layout B, repeated phenomena of crowd breakup and regrouping occur near multiple intersections, forming a network of red and yellow slow-speed zones on the speed diagram, particularly prominent in the 1.0-1.4 m range. After introducing the second exit, the terminal convergence pressure in Layout B2 was not significantly reduced, with only slight decreases in evacuation time, and even increases in time observed in the condition of 1.2m and 1.4-1.8m corridor width. The reason may be that multiple intersection points are still retained in the upper zone of the area, requiring agents to make multiple detours and reorganizations. The two-sided flow distribution and widened passages cannot effectively alleviate congestion at these local intersection points. Meanwhile, the wider passages may actually make agents' avoidance behavior more pronounced, thereby slowing down evacuation time. This indicates that for geometries with dense intersections, although double exits can alleviate terminal congestion, further reduction of low-speed zones still requires local geometric modification measures.

In single-exit Layout C, the parallel chnnels in the upper zone can form orderly queues in advance, but continuous low-speed zones appear at the corner. After setting up double exits, the queues formed in the upper zone can be released directly through the upper-left exit without needing to pass through the corner and merge into the main corridor. This allows the Layout C2 to have the lowest overall evacuation time across all widths. This indicates that the double exits effectively weakened the influence of corner and fully utilized the advantages of parallel channels.
## 4.2 Big Prototype
通过Small Prototype的实验，我们可以得知撤离时间与主车道最终收敛有着显著关系，并且人流的交叉以及并道现象对撤离时间也有着巨大影响。因此在Big Prototype下，我们希望验证它们在较大尺度下的宏观效应，以探测能够最大程度影响撤离时间的阈值区间。
Based on the results from the Small Prototype, evacuation time shows significant correlation with the convergence intensity at the end of main corridors, as well as mutual interference among crowds at intersections and merging points. Therefore, we introduce the Big Prototype to test these mechanisms in larger spaces and crowd scales, to examine their persistence and increase effects at the macro level, with focus on identifying the gates width and geometric threshold ranges that can most significantly infuence the total evacuation time.
### 4.2.1 Effect of Bottleneck(Gate Width) Before Exit

\subsection{Effect of Bottleneck Width(Gate Width Before Exit)}
In the Big Prototype scenario, according to the figure \ref{fig:tet_vs_Bottleneck}, as the exit bottleneck width gradually increased from 1.0 m to 1.6 m, the Total Evacuation Time (TET) showed a continuous declining monotonic trend. The readings approximately showed 85 s at 1.0 m, 75 s at 1.2 m, 73 s at 1.4 m, and 70 s at 1.6 m. The most significant improvement occurred in the interval from 1.0 m to 1.2 m, where TET decreased by approximately 10 s with nearly a twelve percent relative improvement; thereafter, continued widening only brought gradual improvements, with approximately 2 s reduction in the 1.2 m to 1.4 m interval and approximately 3 s reduction in the 1.4 m to 1.6 m interval. Error bars showed that the variability of repeated runs was greater at narrower exits and significantly converged at wider exits, indicating that the system was more stable at larger widths. The slope of the line showed a noticeable change around 1.2 m, suggesting that this point may be the threshold interval for transitioning from long queue formation to short-term congestion, beyond which marginal benefits gradually diminish.

The speed trajectory diagrams are consistent with the aforementioned trends. At the bottleneck width of 1.0 m, the low-speed red zones significantly extend backward, showing queuing phenomena. Due to the queuing phenomenon, agents exhibit obvious convergence behavior at an early stage, beginning to merge lanes with frequent lateral interactions and substantial velocity fluctuations. When the width increases to 1.2 m, the low-speed zone noticeably diminishs and becomes concentrated within several meters of the room exits, as well as the merging points be closer to the room exit. The agents' queuing phenomenon visibly disappears during actual simulation observation. On expansion to 1.4 m and 1.6 m, the low-speed zone continues to diminish and remains close to the doorway, with only brief delays occurring during peak arrival periods, more uniform velocity distribution, and reduced lateral conflicts. The multiple simulations show that the main corridor and overall paths leading to each exit remain consistent, while width variations not triggering large-scale route diversions. The most significant differences are concentrated in the flow microstructure within the final several meters of the exit area.
### 4.2.2 Coupling Effect of Room Door Width and Corridor Gate Width
![[tet_seaborn_heatmap_multiroom.png]]
横轴为 corridor gate width（记为 Wc​），纵轴为 room door width（记为 Wr​），两者的取值水平均为 1.0、1.2、1.4、1.6 m。在每个 (Wr,Wc) 组合下，保持人群规模、布局与行为参数不变，进行多次独立仿真，统计总撤离时间（TET）的均值与标准差并绘制成热力图。

在所有组合中，最短的 TET 为 69.7 s，出现在 Wc=1.4 与 Wr=1.2；最长的 TET 为 89.5 s，出现在 Wc=1.0 与 Wr=1.4。随着走廊门宽增加到 1.4 m 及以上，各组合的 TET 明显收敛到约 70–72 s，且标准差普遍降至约 1–2 s，表明系统运行进入稳定状态。特别值得注意的是，在走廊门较窄（1.0 或 1.2 m）时，单独增大房门宽会显著拖慢撤离时间；这一现象与速度—轨迹图中的并道冲突和排队回溯一致，机制将在后续专门结合轨迹图进行展开。


当房门宽固定为 1.0 m 时，四幅速度—轨迹图清楚地展示了随着走廊门从 1.0 m 逐步加宽到 1.6 m，拥堵的空间分布如何演化，并直接对应了热力图第一行的均值与标准差变化。在 1.0_1.0 的组合下，主走廊和汇入节点附近出现成片的红色低速带，回溯到上游数米，说明并道冲突频繁、队列持续且长，这正对应了热力图中较高的撤离时间。将走廊门增至 1.2 m 后，红带明显收缩，慢速点主要被压缩在关键转角与门槛位置，主通道上的绿色连续段更长，换道与交织次数减少，因此总时间显著下降。进一步增大到 1.4 m 时，慢速区继续贴边、变短，汇入点附近形成稳定的多车道式队列，通行几乎不再对上游产生可见回溯，这与热力图在该列达到行内最短时间相吻合。继续加宽至 1.6 m 时，轨迹形态与 1.4 m 已非常接近，低速点零散且局限于个别几何拐角，系统表现更加可预测，从而带来更小的标准差；与此同时，由于房门仍是系统的供给上限，走廊门的额外加宽不再带来有效吞吐提升，于是均值只在 1.4 m 的基础上略有回升，整体仍处于同一稳定台阶。

Figure xx shows that when room door width is 1.0m, the spatial distribution of congestion evolves as the corridor gate width gradually widens from 1.0m to 1.6m, and directly correspond to the changes in the mean TET and standard deviation of the first row of the heat map. Under the combo of 1.0m and 1.0m, large areas of low-speed zones appear near the main corridor and the merging points. Backtracking several meters upstream, this indicates frequent parallel conflicts, long and continuous queues, which precisely correspond to the relatively high evacuation time in the heat map. After increasing the corridor door to 1.2 meters, the red band shrank significantly. The slow points were mainly compressed at corners and threshold positions. The green continuous section on the main passage became longer, and the number of lane changes and confliction decreased. Therefore, the total time dropped down greatly. When further increased the door width to 1.4m, the slow zone continues to be close to the edge and shortens, forming a stable multi-lane queue near the convergence point. The passage almost no longer causes visible backtracking to the upstream, which is consistent with the heat map reaching the shortest time within the row in this column. When further widened to 1.6m, the trajectory shape is already very close to 1.4m. The low-speed points are scattered and limited to individual geometric corners. The system performance is more predictable, thereby resulting in a smaller standard deviation. Meanwhile, as the door remains the upper limit of the system's supply, the additional widening of the corridor door no longer leads to an increase in effective throughput. As a result, the average value only slightly rises from 1.4m, and the overall situation remains at the same stable level.

According to Figure efgh, keep the room door width as 1.2 m, if the corridor gate width is only 1.0 m, a large low-speed zone will appear before the merging points, with queues continuously backtracking upstream. Because the downstream release capacity is restricted by the gate width, agents can exit smoothly through the room doors, but are forced to stagnate before the narrow corridor gates, which causes longer evacuation time. As it comes to 1.2m, the low-speed zone is compressed near the gate, and the green zone in the main corridor become longer, significantly reducing the total evacuation time. When the corridor gate width reaches 1.4m, the downstream release capacity essentially matches the supply from upstream, backtracking almost disappears and only several low-speed points occur at some corners, therefore the evacuation time drops to its lowest and variation among the runs converges. Even when the corridor gate width is widened to 1.6m, the agents flow pattern is almost the same as that at 1.4m, because the real limitation is the 1.2m room door width, so the TET will not decrease more. However, since congestion becomes more difficult to form, results are more stable and the standard deviation will decrease further. To summerize, when the room door width is 1.2 m, simply increasing the corridor door from 1.0 m to 1.4 m can basicly eliminate queuing, allow the mean TET to drop rapidly and help the system to become stable, while continued widening would not benefit more in stability.



The results indicate that the release capacity of corridor gates plays a dominant role in efficiency and stability. When corridor width larger than 1.4 m, the downstream release capacity matches the supply from upstream, reducing conflicts and queuing, and resulting in similar evacuation time and very small variance. In Contrast, when corridor doors are narrow while room doors are wide, the crowd upstream is released too rapidly and the downstream is restricted, therefore leading to stronger confliction and backward queuing in the merging area.
